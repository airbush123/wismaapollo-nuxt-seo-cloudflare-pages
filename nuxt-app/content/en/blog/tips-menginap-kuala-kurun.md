Kuala Kurun is the capital of Gunung Mas Regency in Central Kalimantan. Visitors come for many reasons: government affairs, field work, family visits, local events, transit, and travel. Whatever the purpose, choosing the right accommodation will strongly affect the comfort of your stay.

This guide explains practical tips for staying in Kuala Kurun so you can choose a hotel, guest house, or lodging that matches your needs.

## 1. Choose a Strategic Location

Location should be the first consideration. A strategic place to stay makes it easier to reach offices, food places, shops, main roads, and local contacts. If your schedule is busy, staying near the town center can save time and energy.

Wisma Apollo is located at Jl. Letjen Soeprapto No.56, Kuala Kurun. The address is easy to share and practical for guests who need access to town activity.

## 2. Check Essential Facilities

Do not focus only on the room rate. Make sure the accommodation provides facilities that support proper rest. Important facilities include AC, WiFi, private bathroom, shower, towels, comfortable bed, parking, and clear communication with the property.

At Wisma Apollo, rooms are equipped with AC, free WiFi, Android TV, private bathroom, towels, amenities, drinking water, and comfortable bedding. These facilities are suitable for work trips, family visits, and transit stays.

## 3. Match Room Type With Guest Count

Before booking, check how many people will stay in the room. A Single Bed room may be enough for solo travelers or simple work trips. A Double Bed room may be more comfortable for guests who need more sleeping space.

If you bring family or a small group, ask the admin about capacity and additional needs such as extra bed or breakfast. Clear information prevents confusion during check-in.

## 4. Confirm the Price and What Is Included

Transparent pricing helps you manage travel budget. Ask whether the price includes basic facilities and whether there are additional charges for special requests. At Wisma Apollo, room rates start from IDR 200,000 per night for Single Bed rooms and IDR 250,000 per night for Double Bed rooms.

For company trips or official duty, clear pricing can also help with administration and reporting.

## 5. Book Before Arriving

It is better to check availability before reaching Kuala Kurun, especially during busy periods or workdays. Direct booking through WhatsApp allows you to confirm the date, room type, number of guests, arrival time, and payment instructions.

This is useful because regional travel schedules can change. If you arrive late or need to adjust plans, direct communication helps the property understand your situation.

## 6. Prepare for Weather and Travel Distance

Central Kalimantan weather can feel hot, and road trips can be tiring. Choose accommodation where you can cool down, shower, and rest properly after arrival. AC, private bathroom, and a clean bed become very important after long travel.

If you plan to visit natural attractions around Gunung Mas, make sure you also prepare footwear, drinking water, and enough time to return before night.

## 7. Ask About Nearby Food and Daily Needs

A good stay is not only about the room. You also need access to food, coffee, shops, and transportation. Before booking, ask whether the accommodation is close to daily needs. Staying near the city center makes this easier.

Wisma Apollo's location in Kuala Kurun helps guests find local food and town services without complicated travel.

## 8. Prioritize Cleanliness and Rest Quality

Cleanliness is one of the most important factors in choosing accommodation. A clean room, fresh towels, and comfortable bedding can improve sleep quality. If you travel for work, good rest helps you perform better the next day.

Sound and comfort also matter. Choose a place where you can recover after a full day of activity.
