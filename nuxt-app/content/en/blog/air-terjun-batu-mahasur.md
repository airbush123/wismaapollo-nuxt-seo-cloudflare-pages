# Batu Mahasur Waterfall

Batu Mahasur Waterfall is one of the hidden natural gems in Gunung Mas Regency, Central Kalimantan. Located not far from Kuala Kurun, this waterfall offers breathtaking natural beauty.

## Location & Access
Batu Mahasur Waterfall is located in the Gunung Mas Regency area. From Kuala Kurun, you can reach this location by a land journey of about 1-2 hours.

## Tips for Visiting
- **Best time**: Visit during the dry season for clear water.
- **Preparation**: Bring a change of clothes, comfortable footwear, and food/drinks.
- **Safety**: Always be careful around the waterfall area.
