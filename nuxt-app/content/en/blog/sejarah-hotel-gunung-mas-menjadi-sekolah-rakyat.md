<p>Kuala Kurun, the capital of Gunung Mas Regency, Central Kalimantan, continues to improve and carve new history in its education sector. One of the most captivating transformation stories recently is the conversion of the oldest hotel building in Gunung Mas into a noble educational facility: the <strong>People's School (Sekolah Rakyat)</strong>.</p>
<p>The building in question was formerly widely known as Hotel Gunung Mas. For years, this building served as one of the earliest accommodation providers for travelers or government officials passing through Kuala Kurun. This place recorded many traces of the city's economic development history. However, as time passed and the local government became aware of the importance of educational equality, this historic hotel was closed and fully transformed into a facility for educating the nation's next generation.</p>
<h2>The People's School Program in Kuala Kurun</h2>
<p>The Gunung Mas Regency Government's decision to establish a People's School was not without reason. Many children from remote or deep rural areas in Central Kalimantan are at risk of dropping out of school or struggling to access regular education due to costs and travel distances. To break the chain of poverty and give them equal opportunities, the Pioneer People's School initiative was launched in mid-2025.</p>
<p>Instead of building foundations from scratch, which would require enormous time and cost, the government cleverly rehabilitated the former Hotel Gunung Mas building. Rooms that were once rented to guests are now converted into classrooms and boarding school dormitories for underprivileged students. The hotel's dining room was transformed into a communal learning space and multipurpose hall.</p>
<h2>Positive Impact for Children from Remote Areas</h2>
<p>This integrated boarding facility is crucial for students who come from far away from the Kuala Kurun city center. They are not only given free basic education, but also safe accommodation, daily meals, and adequate uniforms. Elementary to junior high school curricula serve as the initial pilot phase before the government plans a more spacious permanent building.</p>
<p>The transformation decision from a commercial hotel to a school has garnered much admiration. This step proves that Gunung Mas Regency prioritizes education above all else. The remnants of the past-crossing building are now surrounded by laughter and new hopes of students who pin their high aspirations.</p>
<h2>The Meaning of Transformation for Kuala Kurun's City Face</h2>
<p>The change of function of an old building often carries a bigger message than just physical renovation. In Kuala Kurun, a building once closely associated with guest activities and travel is now linked to educational hope. Stories like this show that a city doesn't only grow through roads, markets, and economic facilities, but also through social decisions that directly impact the young generation.</p>
<p>For local residents, old buildings usually store collective memories. Some remember it as a stopover point, meeting place, or part of a family journey. When the building transforms into a learning space, old memories don't simply disappear. It gains a new, broader function: becoming a place where children learn, live, grow, and prepare for a better future.</p>
