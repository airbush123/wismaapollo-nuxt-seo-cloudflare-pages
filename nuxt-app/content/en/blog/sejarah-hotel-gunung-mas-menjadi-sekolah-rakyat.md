# Gunung Mas Oldest Hotel Transformation

Extraordinary history records the transformation of one of the oldest hotels in Gunung Mas Regency, which has now been repurposed into a "Sekolah Rakyat" (People's School). This step was taken to provide educational access for children in the remote areas of Central Kalimantan.

This school serves as a testament to the importance of utilizing local assets for community progress.
